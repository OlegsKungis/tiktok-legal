Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

1. Overview

UploadV6 ("the App") is a personal tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

2. Data the App accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit), the App receives an access token and the basic profile information of the signed-in account (such as display name), using the permissions the owner approves: user.info.basic and video.upload (and video.publish if direct posting is enabled).

3. How data is used

The access token is used only to upload the owner's videos and to check the status of those uploads. The App does not collect, analyse or sell data about any other TikTok users.

4. Storage

The token and upload history are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The App has no server and does not transmit this data anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

5. Sharing

No data is shared with, or sold to, third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until deleted. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and can delete the locally stored token file.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process their data.
